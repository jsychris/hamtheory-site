Frequently asked questions

Is this an official RSGB or Ofcom app?

No. Foundation Ham is an independent study aid. It isn't made by or affiliated with the RSGB (who set and administer the exam) or Ofcom (who issue the licence). Its questions and lessons are original, written from the freely published RSGB syllabus learning points and the Ofcom licence conditions. Always check rsgb.org for exam booking, fees and the current syllabus.

How closely do the mock exams match the real exam?

The Full Mock uses the real format: 26 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes with a pass mark of 19, drawn from every syllabus section each time. Quick Mock, Smart Practice and the Dynamic Exam let you practise in shorter sessions at the same pass standard.

Can I use a calculator, like in the real exam?

In the real exam a silent, battery-powered, non-programmable calculator is allowed (the invigilator has the final say). A few Foundation questions involve a one-step calculation such as Ohm's law, so it's worth practising with one — the app's timed mocks are a good place to build that habit.

How do I book the real exam?

Exams are booked through the RSGB, and most candidates sit them online with remote invigilation. See rsgb.org for booking, current fees and what to expect on the day.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. Everything works offline, there's no sign-up, and the app has no adverts or tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I change phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, so it isn't synced between devices. It is kept in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup to a new device should bring it with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Profile → Reset all progress and confirm. This clears your XP, streaks, badges, completed lessons and exam history. It can't be undone.

How up to date is the content?

The content targets RSGB syllabus v1.6b (exams from April 2025) and the licence conditions introduced in February 2024 — 25 W for Foundation, M7 callsigns, optional regional locators. Syllabus and licence conditions do change, so check rsgb.org before you sit. If you think something is out of date or wrong, please email drivingtheoryapps@gmail.com.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
